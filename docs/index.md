## About this wiki

### Who is this for?

Beginners who want to learn by doing.

### What are the prerequisites?

1. A few months of experience with the basics.
2. A browser and a willingness to learn (the most important one).

### How do I approach this?

1. **Code along.** Type the examples yourself.
2. **Explore and experiment.** Change things and see what breaks.
3. **Do the exercises.** You will get stuck, and that's normal.
4. **Share your work.** Teaching others is the best way to learn.

### Do I need to take things in order?

The chapters build on each other, but feel free to jump around.

Happy learning!
